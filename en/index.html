Travel to Hanoi

01

Before you fly

Visa requirements depend on your nationality. ASEAN, Japanese, and South Korean passport holders are among those who may enter visa-free.

Travelers who need a visa - including many US, Canadian, and Australian passport holders - can apply through Vietnam’s official e-visa portal a few weeks ahead.

Official e-visa portal ↗
02

Arriving in Hanoi

Fly into Noi Bai International Airport (HAN). In the arrivals hall, you can exchange cash and buy a local SIM card.

Viettel and Vinaphone are recommended for reliable data coverage.

04

Paying in Hanoi

Cash is still preferred at markets, street-food stalls, local cafés, and small shops. Carry smaller 20,000–100,000 VND notes for everyday purchases.

Visa and Mastercard are widely accepted at hotels, upscale restaurants, malls, and established convenience stores. ATMs and official exchange counters are readily available.

Late December in Hanoi is usually cool, around 18–23°C (64–73°F). The vow ceremony will be outside, so please bring a light jacket.

Gifts are welcome but not required. Your presence is also our gift ♥
